Our Story

A Culinary Journey Born from Passion

Founded in 2018, Ember & Oak represents the culmination of Chef Marcus Thornton's twenty-year journey through the world's most prestigious kitchens.

We believe in the transformative power of fire and the timeless beauty of natural materials. Our name reflects our commitment to wood-fired cooking techniques and sustainable, locally-sourced ingredients.
