Our Story

A Culinary Journey Born from Passion

Founded in 2018, Ember & Oak represents the culmination of Chef Marcus Thornton's twenty-year journey through the world's most prestigious kitchens. From Paris to Tokyo, New York to Barcelona, each destination has influenced our unique approach to modern fine dining.

We believe in the transformative power of fire and the timeless beauty of natural materials. Our name reflects our commitment to wood-fired cooking techniques and sustainable, locally-sourced ingredients that honor both tradition and innovation.

Every dish that leaves our kitchen is a testament to our philosophy: respect for ingredients, mastery of technique, and an unwavering dedication to creating memorable experiences.
